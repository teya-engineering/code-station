The avatar-moods-*.png files use the seeds code-station-moods-01 through
code-station-moods-64. They are generated as 128 by 128 pixel PNG files with the
DiceBear 10.x HTTP API. The app bundles these files and makes no runtime network
requests to DiceBear.
